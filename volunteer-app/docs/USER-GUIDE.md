# Using the MINDS Volunteer App

For volunteers and MINDS staff. Setup and code are covered in the [README](../README.md).

**One app for everyone.** Every volunteer, MINDS staff included, signs in with their own
account and sees **Home · Pass · Schedule · Trainees · Me**. MINDS staff open the
**Admin** menu with the shared MINDS admin password (**Me → Admin**, or **Open admin** in
the menu on a computer). Admin then stays open on that device for 12 hours, until you tap
the 🔒 icon (**Leave admin**). Everything done in Admin is recorded with your name.

The same pages work on a phone and a computer. On a phone the menu is tabs under the
header, and the admin sections are a row of chips you can swipe; on a computer the menu is
on the left. The header's ↻ button loads the newest version of the app and 🌗 switches
dark mode.

## For volunteers

| I want to… | Tap |
| --- | --- |
| Join | **Register now**, fill in your details and your **Telegram handle**, agree to the data notice, **Create account** |
| Get started | Read the Volunteer Guide that opens, then **I've read the guide** (needed once, before your first session) |
| Say I'm coming | **Schedule** → tick every session you can come to (e.g. all four this month) → **I'm coming to N sessions**. There is nothing to wait for: you're on the list |
| Share the schedule with a caregiver | **Schedule** → **Share** (or take a screenshot of the page) |
| Say I can't make it | **Home** → Coming up → **Can't make it** (on the day, tell MINDS staff instead) |
| See who I'm with | **Home**: your trainee(s) for each session. **Trainees** for more about them |
| Read about a trainee | **Trainees** → name. Regular volunteers see every trainee; new volunteers see the trainee(s) they are paired with |
| Update a trainee after a visit | Trainee page → **Add an update** → change any answer that is different now, and/or write a note → **Send update** |
| Clock in and out | **Pass** → show it to MINDS staff at the start, and again at the end |
| Give feedback | **Home** → **Give feedback** after the session: a 1–5 rating, comments, and notes for future volunteers |
| Update my details | **Me** → My details → **Edit** → **Save changes** |
| Change my password | **Me** → Change password |
| Forgot my password | Ask a MINDS admin to reset it |
| Re-read the guide | **Me** → Volunteer Guide |

Good to know:
- **New and regular volunteers.** You start as a new volunteer; an admin pairs you with a
  trainee before each session. After **10 completed sessions** you become a regular
  volunteer automatically: you are paired straight away (with your regular trainees if you
  have them) and see more about every trainee. **Home** and **Me** show how many sessions
  are left.
- **Your pass works without signal.** The app keeps passes for your sessions over the next
  two weeks on your phone. Turn the brightness up if the scan is slow.
- **A pass only works on its own day**, so a screenshot from another day is rejected.
  Open the pass in the app instead.
- **Changing your email** changes how you sign in, so the app asks for your current
  password first.
- **Your notes about a trainee** are read by an admin before other volunteers see them.
  Answers you change in **Add an update** go straight into MINDS's house-visit records,
  with your name.

## For MINDS staff: the Admin menu

Open it from **Me → Admin** (phone) or **Open admin** in the left menu (computer), and type
the MINDS admin password. A wrong password is recorded too; after 5 wrong tries you wait 15
minutes.

**Sessions & pairing**

| I want to… | Tap |
| --- | --- |
| See who's coming | **Sessions & pairing** → a session. **Need a trainee** (new and ad-hoc volunteers) is at the top; regulars are usually paired already |
| Pair someone | Their row → **Pair** (or **Change**) → tick every trainee they look after → **Save**. A trainee can be with more than one volunteer, and a volunteer with more than one trainee |
| Pair everyone left | **Pair the rest automatically** (it uses free trainees; if none are left, pair by hand) |
| See a volunteer's Telegram or phone | **Press and hold** their name (right-click on a computer) |
| Take someone off a session | Their row → **Remove** |
| Add a session | **Sessions & pairing** → **New session** → **Type** (Volunteer session or Outing), title (optional), date, times, venue, project, slots, notes, trainees attending → **Add session**. Everyone sees it in their Schedule straight away |
| Use a new venue | In the Venue box type its name → **+ Add a new venue** |
| Edit, close or cancel a session | Session → **Edit or close this session** (to be reviewed with MINDS in person) |

**On the day**

| I want to… | Tap |
| --- | --- |
| Clock volunteers in and out | **Scan & clock in** → **Scan QR passes** → point the camera at their pass. First scan = in, next = out |
| Someone turns up without saying they're coming | Scan them → **Add as walk-in and clock in** (choose the session at the top if there are several today) |
| A volunteer's phone is flat | **Scan & clock in** → **Clock in by hand** → choose the session → **Clock in** / **Clock out** |
| No signal | Keep scanning. Scans are saved on your phone and upload by themselves later (or tap **Upload now**) |

**Pairing list (regular pairs)**

| I want to… | Tap |
| --- | --- |

| See it the other way round | **By trainee** (trainees without a regular volunteer are listed too) |
| End a pair | Tap **×** on it (it stays as history) |
| Bring in existing pairs | **Import from house-visit responses** or **Import from the pairs sheet** (link them first under **Settings → Databases**). Names that don't match anyone in the app are listed |

**Guide and settings**

| I want to… | Tap |
| --- | --- |
| Edit the Volunteer Guide | **Guide & venues** → **Edit** on a section, or **Add section**; arrows reorder |
| Show a picture or PDF in the guide | **Guide & venues** → **Add picture/PDF** (or **Replace picture/PDF**) on the section. "On the day" starts with a built-in infographic |

## How the app decides things

- **Pairing.** When someone says they're coming: if they have regular pairs (Pairing
  list), they get all of those trainees. Otherwise regular volunteers get a free trainee
  (only from the session's "Trainees attending" if set, never one kept for their own
  regular partner who is also coming): the one they were with last time, else the one with
  the fewest pairings so far. New volunteers wait for an admin. Trainees marked
  `Active: No` are never paired automatically.
- **Regular volunteers.** "Volunteer Type" in the Volunteers tab: blank means automatic
  (regular after 10 completed sessions, written as "Regular" with the date); admins can
  also set Regular or New.
- **No-shows.** Someone said they were coming to a session that is now over (before
  today) and never clocked in. Cancelled sessions and "Can't make it" don't count.
- **Scans.** The first scan clocks a volunteer in and the next clocks them out. A repeat
  scan within 2 minutes is ignored. Hours are written to the sheet immediately.
- **What volunteers see** is set in **Settings**. Medical and caregiver details start as
  admins only (medical and dietary notes are visible to regulars). House-visit answers are
  hidden until an admin allows them.
- **Admin access.** One shared admin password, not tied to anyone. It is stored only as a
  hash. Changing it ends admin on every other device. You can't deactivate your own
  account.
